The IBM | HBCU Quantum Center is a partnership between IBM and Howard University that cultivates diverse talent in the rapidly evolving field of quantum computing. Unlike many industries with entrenched hiring biases, quantum computing offers an inclusive environment where people from all backgrounds can thrive. As a new entity on the Howard campus, the center needed a brand and visual identity to expand its reach across HBCUs and support its goals.

As creative director, I led a unique project that combined student talent with IBM Design Thinking to develop a groundbreaking brand for the HBCU Quantum Center. Collaborating with student designers, we created a brand that embodies opportunity and equity, reflecting the energy and potential of a new generation in this transformative field.

Challenge

When the IBM | HBCU Quantum Center moved beyond the Howard University name and brand, it was left without a distinct identity or the brand support needed to build recognition. Since its inception, the organization lacked a cohesive visual system to create consistent communications and experiences across its growing network of HBCUs, students, researchers, and industry partners.

Solution

Build a distinct brand that could represent the HBCU Quantum Center’s broader mission: advancing the impact of Black people in quantum computing. The identity needed to bring consistency to the organization while expressing its commitment to mentorship, scholarship, and expertise—and its ambition to develop the next generation of leaders in the field.

Approach

IBM Quantum Design partnered with a group of Howard University students to develop the brand through a series of design sprints aligned with their fall and spring academic semesters. I guided the students through the principles of enterprise branding, marketing design, and product design, creating a collaborative process that paired practical instruction with the work of building a functioning brand. Together, we developed an identity and design foundation the organization could use to communicate consistently and grow beyond a single university.

Outcome

The HBCU Quantum Center gained a distinct brand foundation to support its mission and represent a program created for the broader HBCU community. The collaboration also gave participating students hands-on experience applying enterprise branding, marketing, and product design principles to a real organization—helping prepare them to contribute to the future of the quantum computing industry.
