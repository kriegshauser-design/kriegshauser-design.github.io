Oracle Redwood
Design System

Client
Oracle
Year
2018–2019
Role
Associate Creative Director
Team
Brand & Software Design

Oracle’s extensive portfolio of products and applications lacked a unified visual and functional identity. Redwood brought those experiences together through a flexible, customer-first design language and a shared foundation for design and development.

Project Goal

Redwood established a common visual and functional language for Oracle’s broad product ecosystem. The system was designed to create a recognizable experience across applications while giving individual teams the flexibility to solve the needs of their customers.

Solution

We began with an in-depth review of Oracle’s user interfaces, identifying shared patterns, components, and opportunities for consistency. A week-long collaborative workshop in Seattle brought designers and stakeholders together to explore three creative directions, considering how the system should look, function, and feel. We refined the direction with Oracle executives and select customers, then developed the design language alongside tools that helped teams put it to work.

Challenge

Creating a system at Oracle’s scale required sustained cross-team collaboration, stakeholder alignment, and executive support. We introduced Oracle’s first design wireframe toolkit so teams could prototype and gather feedback before components were finalized, then move from wireframes to high-fidelity compositions with greater speed and consistency. Redwood was unveiled at Oracle OpenWorld 2019, alongside a digital platform that introduced the system and showcased Oracle’s design team and culture.
