Dell Channel Sales App

Client
Dell Technologies
Year
2014–2017
Role
Associate Experience Director
Team
Dell channel sales, UX, and engineering teams

Problem: Channel sales teams needed to build accurate proposals quickly while working face-to-face with customers. Pricing, inventory, and delivery details had to be coordinated in real time, and delays could mean losing a sale.

Approach: Worked with sales teams to design a collaborative app that two people could use together. The experience let salespeople customize orders to customer requests and build a bid in real time, supporting the pace and practical needs of field sales.

Outcomes: The app helped streamline the channel sales proposal process. At launch, sales increased by 8%, contributing to a business channel responsible for a substantial share of Dell's yearly profit.
