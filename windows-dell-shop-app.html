Dell Shop App
for Windows

Client:
Dell
Year:
2012–2015
Role:
Associate Experience Director
Team:
Dell and Microsoft product, UX, and engineering teams

Microsoft approached Dell about collaborating on a shopping app that would come preloaded on Windows 8 PCs. The app was still in development and had not launched, which meant working in a tight iterative sprint with the Microsoft design team to build an experience that felt native to the new OS. The result was a beautiful Windows-native app that allowed users to access the full Dell e-commerce platform in a frictionless in-screen experience.

Challenge

Microsoft introduced a new hybrid version of Windows that unified desktop, tablet, and mobile experiences around a content-driven, primarily horizontal interface called Metro. While this created new opportunities for app design, it also challenged familiar interaction patterns, increased the learning curve, and exposed gaps in how designers could support different screen sizes, input methods, and user contexts.

Solution

Design a Dell Shop experience that made Windows 8 capabilities more accessible while reducing friction for users. Balance familiar shopping behaviors with platform-native interactions, making essential tools, product information, and navigation easier to discover and use.

Approach

Apply Microsoft’s design principles while exploring the platform’s emerging capabilities. Use the native App Bar for universal actions, adapt layouts for Snap View and multiple input methods, and make content an extension of navigation through product counts, category summaries, and contextual information. Combine horizontal and vertical exploration to make better use of screen space and help users understand where they are and what comes next.

Outcome

The project demonstrated how a commerce experience could embrace Windows 8 as a distinct platform rather than simply adapt a traditional website. By aligning with native interaction patterns and making content more discoverable, the design established a more consistent, adaptable, and intuitive foundation for shopping across devices.
