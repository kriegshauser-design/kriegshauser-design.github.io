Dell Wallpaper
Art Direction

Who:
Dell
Year:
2015–2019
Role:
Associate Experience Director
Team:
Dell brand, marketing, and product teams

Developed a global wallpaper and screen-fill strategy that elevated desktop imagery from individual product assets to a cohesive brand-level graphic element. The initiative aligned product, marketing, and e-commerce experiences around a consistent visual language while improving team efficiency and strengthening Dell’s brand presence.

Challenge

The lack of a clear strategy and creative direction for wallpapers and screen fills across Dell products, marketing, and e-commerce increased production costs, created friction between business units, and weakened the customer experience. Inconsistent imagery also made it harder to build a recognizable visual connection between Dell devices and the broader brand.

Solution

Create a consistent global wallpaper and screen-fill strategy with a clear creative vision, uniting internal teams and gaining stakeholder buy-in. Elevate wallpapers to a brand-level graphic element that carries customers seamlessly across the Dell journey, supports franchise recognition, and aligns with Dell’s brand photography standards.

Approach

Usability research and customer-journey insights informed a global wallpaper toolkit. Descriptive themes guided imagery for each franchise, while individual expressions laddered up to a shared brand concept. Locations and image selections were chosen to meet the needs of multiple franchises. Concepts were refreshed on a two-year rotation, with clarity, focus, and sharpness considered essential for sustained desktop use. Wallpapers were designed to build consistent brand awareness, not serve as the sole means of franchise differentiation.

Outcome

The strategy established a more consistent visual experience across Dell devices, marketing, and e-commerce. It gave teams a shared creative framework, reduced duplicated effort and stock photography needs, and strengthened brand recognition across the customer journey while preserving flexibility for individual franchises and screen formats.
