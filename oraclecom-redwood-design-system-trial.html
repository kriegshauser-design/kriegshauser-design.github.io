Oracle.com & Redwood Design System

/ About the project

Oracle.com needed a more consistent digital experience across a complex platform, with a shared visual language that could support marketing and product goals while continuing to evolve the Oracle brand.

As Associate Creative Director, I drove the creation of the Oracle.com Redwood design system and its broader adoption. I partnered with Program Management, Engineering, Test, UX-Ops, and brand and software design teams, while introducing an agile framework to help teams deliver and refine experiences more efficiently.

Established a unified visual language and a repeatable design foundation for Oracle.com. The design system and cross-functional working methods supported greater consistency across the platform and enabled continuous improvement as business and product needs evolved.
