Oracle.com

Client
Oracle
Year
2017
Role
Associate Creative Director, Web/Product
Team
Oracle Web Design Team

Oracle.com was the front door to a vast and evolving portfolio, but its experience had become difficult to navigate and maintain. Distinct site structures, inconsistent patterns, and fragmented content made it harder for customers to find the right information and move confidently through the site.

Working across design, UX, content, and development, we brought greater clarity to the experience—simplifying navigation, improving key journeys, and establishing a more consistent foundation for the site.

Experience:

We focused on making Oracle.com easier to explore across devices. Clearer content hierarchy, more deliberate page structures, and responsive layouts helped people move from broad discovery to the information and products most relevant to them.

Management:

We brought teams together around shared standards and a more coordinated approach to content, design, and delivery. This helped reduce one-off solutions while giving teams a clearer framework for evolving the site.

Approach:

Using an iterative design and development process, we aligned stakeholders, tested ideas early, and refined high-value journeys. A consistent set of patterns made it easier to scale improvements across the broader Oracle.com experience.

Performance:

A more coherent experience gave Oracle a stronger digital foundation—one that supported clearer product discovery, more consistent brand expression, and ongoing optimization across the site.
