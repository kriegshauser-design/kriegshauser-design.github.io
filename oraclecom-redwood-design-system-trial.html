Oracle.com needed a more consistent digital experience across a complex platform, with a shared visual language that could support marketing and product goals while continuing to evolve the Oracle brand.

I led the creation and adoption of the Redwood design system, partnering with Program Management, Engineering, Test, UX-Ops, and brand and software design teams. An agile framework helped teams deliver and refine experiences more efficiently.

Problem:

Bringing brand expression and interface patterns into a cohesive system helped teams build with greater consistency across Oracle.com.

Approach:

Patterns and components were shaped around the needs of teams creating and maintaining digital experiences at scale.

Outcome:

The system created a repeatable foundation for teams to extend, test, and improve the experience as business and product needs changed.
