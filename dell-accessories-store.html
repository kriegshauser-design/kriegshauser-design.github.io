Problem: Dell’s Software and Accessories storefront operated as a transactional catalog built primarily to drive add-on sales alongside PC purchases. Its legacy structure limited product discoverability, comparison, and cross-category exploration, creating an opportunity to evolve the experience into a standalone commerce destination.

Approach: Led the end-to-end redesign of the storefront in 2017, aligning information architecture, UX/UI, and CMS capabilities around customer needs and shopping behaviors. Introduced a streamlined taxonomy and navigation model, enhanced filtering and sorting, structured product comparison, and Dell.com’s first personalized shopping and recommendation system to improve product discovery and decision-making.

Outcomes: Transformed a legacy upsell catalog into a more intuitive, personalized shopping experience, strengthening the path from product discovery to purchase. The redesigned platform delivered an immediate 35% year-over-year increase in sales following launch.
